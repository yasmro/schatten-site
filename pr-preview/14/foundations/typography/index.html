Typography

Each role (Heading, Body, Label) bundles font size, line height and weight into one .st-text class. Apply it through Text, so text is styled the same way everywhere.

Font families

  • The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.素早い茶色の狐が怠惰な犬を飛び越える。--font-sans · Hanken Grotesk / LINE Seed JPThe default for UI and body text. Load both faces yourself; otherwise it falls back to the system sans stack. LINE Seed JP has no 500 or 600 weight, so Japanese glyphs use the nearest available weight.
  • The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.素早い茶色の狐が怠惰な犬を飛び越える。--font-serif · EB Garamond / Noto Serif JPEditorial serif, used through <Text family="serif">. Load weights 400 to 700 yourself: a missing weight renders as synthetic bold.
  • The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.--font-mono · System monospaceFor code and technical content.

Japanese and Latin sizes

At the same pixel size, Latin faces look smaller than Japanese faces, which fill their em box. Schatten applies font-size-adjust: cap-height library-wide to lift Latin glyphs to the Japanese height. Tune or turn it off with --st-font-size-adjust.

Type scale

Heading

Page titles, section headers and card headings. Semibold or bold, with snug or tight leading.

  • Display titlesheading-2xl
  • Page titlesheading-xl
  • Section titlesheading-lg
  • Subsection titlesheading-md
  • Card titlesheading-sm

Body

Paragraphs, descriptions and general content. Normal weight, with relaxed leading for readability.

  • Lead paragraphs and introductionsbody-lg
  • Default body textbody-md
  • Secondary text and descriptionsbody-sm
  • Captions and footnotesbody-xs

Label

Form labels, buttons, navigation and badges. Medium weight, with tight leading.

  • Navigation itemslabel-lg
  • Form labels and buttonslabel-md
  • Tags and badgeslabel-sm
  • Overlines and micro labelslabel-xs

Primitives

The values behind the roles. Do not apply them to your own elements: go through Text.
