Motion

Enter and exit timing comes from a three-step duration scale. Pick a duration by what the motion is for, not by a millisecond count.

Duration scale

Three steps that are perceptually distinct. Run the transition to compare them: the faster knob settles first.

--st-duration-fast
100ms
--st-duration-base
150ms
--st-duration-slow
200ms

By intent

The --motion-* tokens are named aliases of the steps. They are defined only: hover transitions are still hardcoded in components, and enter and exit use --st-duration-*. The values carry no easing, so specify your own.

  • --motion-quickHover and exit: light feedback that should feel near-instant
    Defined only
  • --motion-baseThe default for most enter transitions
    Defined only
  • --motion-expressiveDialog and drawer enter: larger surfaces read as deliberate when a little slower
    Defined only

Principles

  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Every animated component turns motion off under it, and new motion must do the same.
  • Do not animate without a reason. If a transition does not help the user see what changed, leave it out.
  • Continuous loops such as the Spinner use timing of their own, not this scale.
